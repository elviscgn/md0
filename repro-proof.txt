Reproducible build check
========================

Commands:

go build -trimpath -buildvcs=false -ldflags="-s -w -buildid=" -o /tmp/md0-a ./cmd/md0
go build -trimpath -buildvcs=false -ldflags="-s -w -buildid=" -o /tmp/md0-b ./cmd/md0
sha256sum /tmp/md0-a /tmp/md0-b

Observed on Go 1.23.2 linux/amd64:

9fae42a55616038195f1a06c0f326ece2c03cea55c06f55eec954967bf414363  /tmp/md0-a
9fae42a55616038195f1a06c0f326ece2c03cea55c06f55eec954967bf414363  /tmp/md0-b

The two artifacts were byte-identical in this environment.
